How to Play

  • 📷 Each day brings 4 photos from one of three categories. Guess the numeric value — capacity, height, or year of origin.
  • 🎛️ Use the slider or type your guess directly into the number fields with your keyboard.
  • 🎯 The closer your guess, the more points you earn! Up to 1000 points per photo.
  • 📅 A new round every day. Missed one? Browse past rounds in the Calendar.
  • 📊 Track your progress and share your scores with friends.
